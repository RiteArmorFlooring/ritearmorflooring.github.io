Installation overview

RiteArmor® urethane concrete systems are available as 3-component or 4-component products and are designed for practical field installation.

Substrate treatment

Mechanically treat the substrate to remove laitance, surface contamination, and friable material, producing a clean, sound, textured surface. Anchor grooves are required at free edges.

Priming

Prime and seal the substrate completely. Defects such as blow holes should be repaired before overlaying, and anchor grooves should be filled.

Apply urethane concrete

Mix the components, spread the mixture evenly with a rake or notched trowel, and use a spiked roller to remove air bubbles and create a smooth, even finish.
